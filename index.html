    Hand tracking runs on your device. The video never leaves your browser.

    How to play

    🫰 Snap
    Wake the particles, or send them back to sleep
    ✊ Fist
    Build the model; from apart, put it back together
    ✋ Open hand
    Pull the parts apart. How wide you open sets how far
    🤚 Tilt
    Tilt your hand left or right to spin the model
    ✌️ Peace
    Next model
    ☝️ Point
    Point at a part to see what it does
    👋 Move
    While the particles swirl, they follow your hand

    Use your own model

    Drag a .glb file onto the page, or choose Open 3D file. Each named object in the file becomes a part you can pull apart. Blender, Sketchfab and most 3D tools can export .glb. The file stays on your device.
