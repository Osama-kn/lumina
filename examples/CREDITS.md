# Example model credits

The bundled 3D models come from the [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets). They were reduced with `scripts/slim-glb.mjs`: textures removed and triangles thinned. The part names, groupings and explanations in the `.json` files are Lumina's.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chess-set.glb` | [A Beautiful Game](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ABeautifulGame) | MaterialX Project (ASWF), 2020. Converted to glTF by Ed Mackey, 2022 | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `flight-helmet.glb` | [Flight Helmet](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/FlightHelmet) | Gary Hsu, 2018 | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `refrigerator.glb` | [Commercial Refrigerator](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CommercialRefrigerator) | Eric Chadwick (Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH), 2025, based on [Commercial Fridge](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/commercial-fridge-2174e1e4f1f24f1a95aa110ee060f473) by Sean Thomas | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

Changes: geometry simplified, textures and materials other than base colour removed, objects grouped into named parts.
